Section III · Process

From commit to cover story, in three steps.

Drop a repo URL once. PushPad takes care of the rest — crawling, scoring, ranking, and gently nagging you to write the weekly update.

i.

Claim your project.

Paste a GitHub URL. We pull the README, OG image, latest release tag, and your stack from the manifest. You confirm the title, the one-line tagline, and the hero color. About a minute, total.

fig. 3.1 — claim flowstep i.
ii.

We auto-crawl in the background.

Every 4 minutes, our crawler hits the GitHub release API and your OG image. New tag → new entry. New OG → cover refresh. We never touch private repos or read tokens.

fig. 3.2 — crawl orbitstep ii.
iii.

You climb the board.

Every release tag minted in the last 7 days adds to your velocity. Post a one-line update on Friday and you'll get a small visibility boost in the weekend digest. Featured slots are admin-curated.

fig. 3.3 — the climbstep iii.
Section IV · The Readership

A board for three kinds of builder.

— Reader

The visitor, browsing.

Curious devs, hackernews scrollers, students looking for stack ideas, recruiters pretending not to be recruiters. Browse the board, drill into a project, copy its tag stack into your next side-project.

  • No login required for reading.
  • Filter by stack: "projects using Bun + Postgres".
  • RSS feed of the weekly top 10.
  • Saved boards if you do log in.
— Creator

The creator, shipping.

Indiehackers actually building. You claim your project, sync your repo, and post a one-line "what shipped this week". Crawler does the rest. The leaderboard quietly motivates you to push on Sunday night.

  • One repo per claim. Multi-claim for orgs.
  • Auto-imports release notes & tags.
  • Weekly write-up nudge via email.
  • Friction-free badge for your own README.
— Editor / Admin

The admin, curating.

That's you, if you're running PushPad. Tune the velocity window, pick a featured project for the week, set the boost multiplier, and ban the occasional spam claim. Like editing a magazine — but the issue ships every Sunday.

  • Velocity window: 1d → 30d.
  • Per-project featured boost (×1.0–×2.0).
  • Manual override on rank ties.
  • Weekly digest scheduling.

Got a project?
Push it up, & let the board do the rest.

Free to claim. Free to climb. Featured slots are editorial and unpaid. Sundays at midnight UTC, the board resets and the week starts again.
