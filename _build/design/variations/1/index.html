By the editors
A correspondence from the build-in-public newsroom.

Register a project, paste a GitHub repo and a domain, and the crawler does the rest. PushPad reads your releases, your CHANGELOG.md, your OG image and your weekly post, then puts you on the board next to everyone else who shipped that week.

The only score that counts is velocity: a decaying sum of releases, commits and human-readable notes over a rolling seven-day window. Burn quietly for three weeks and you fall off the front page. Ship one good Friday and you climb thirty spots before lunch.

No upvotes. No "Show HN" theatre. Just a board, a crawler, and a small editorial team that boosts one project a week — clearly marked, never bought.

The Process — three steps, no calls
Submit a project on Tuesday, ship a release on Friday — find yourself on the front page by Monday morning.
Section II · pp. 2
01claim
Drop your repo and a domain.

Paste your GitHub repository and your live domain. We verify ownership with a .well-known/pushpad file or a single signed DNS TXT — no OAuth dance, no marketplace, no waiting.

You keep the project. PushPad doesn't store your code, only metadata: tags, releases, deploy frequency, the OG card.

02crawl
We read what you ship, automatically.

The crawler sweeps every four minutes. It pulls release tags, commit cadence, your CHANGELOG.md, your OG image, and a single weekly note you write in markdown.

Velocity is computed live: Σ release_weight · e^(-λ·days). No vanity stars. No follower counts. Just shipping.

03climb
Climb the board. Get found.

Ship something good and the velocity score moves you up the board within minutes. Featured projects get a deck slot and a row in the Friday email — read by 11k builders.

No paid placements. The "Editor's pick" is one project a week, chosen by humans, marked clearly.
