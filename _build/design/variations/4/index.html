№ 02 / How it works

Claim. Crawl. Climb.

Three steps · ~2 min total
i.
— You

Claim
your project.

Drop a public GitHub repo URL. Verify ownership with a one-line tag in your README.md. We don't need OAuth, your build pipeline, or your firstborn.

ii.
— PushPad

We auto-crawl
GitHub & OG.

Every Monday 06:00 UTC we re-pull releases, commit cadence, contributor count, and the project's og:image. Public, polite, cached. You change nothing.

iii.
— The Board

Climb the board,
or get out of the way.

Each release pushes you up. Each silent week pushes you down. Post a one-line weekly update for a small bonus and a permalink your customers will actually read.

№ 03 / Who it's for

Three kinds of reader.

Pick your seat.
— I. The Visitor

Watch what's actually shipping.

You scroll PushPad with coffee. The board is a weekly stand-up for the whole indie-software internet — without LinkedIn voice or "thrilled to announce."

  • One page. One sort order. No infinite scroll.
  • RSS firehose, weekly digest, or board widget.
  • Find the next thing to subscribe to before it's on Hacker News.
— II. The Creator

Get credit for the work, weekly.

You ship. PushPad notices. Claim your project, post a one-line weekly update, and turn your release pipeline into compounding signal.

  • Auto-imported releases & commit cadence.
  • Pinned weekly update (140 char), permalinked.
  • Embed your live rank on your own site, in one line.
— III. The Admin

Tune the board. Without breaking trust.

Editors curate featured boosts, the velocity window, and who gets the front-page strap. Every change is logged, public, and timestamped.

  • Adjust velocity window: 7d / 14d / 30d.
  • Featured boosts cap: 3 per week, public log.
  • Spam & abandoned-project review queue.
— Issue 017 closes Monday 06:00 UTC

board.

If you ship anything next week — even a typo fix — PushPad notices. Claim your project now and your first crawl runs at the next window.
